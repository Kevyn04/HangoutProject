The Hangout

Privacy Policy — Last updated June 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how The Hangout ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account

Username — required to create an account. We use a synthetic email address (username@hangout.local) internally for authentication; your real email is never required for standard sign-up.

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive only the information those providers share (typically a name and email address) and use it solely to create your profile.
Profile

Bio, avatar color, profile emoji — optional fields you choose to add. These are visible to other users of the app.
Location

Precise location — collected only when you explicitly enable location sharing inside a Bubble. Location is shared in real time with other members of that Bubble and is not stored long-term after the Bubble ends. You can disable sharing at any time from within the Bubble screen.
Content

Messages, Bubbles, Events, Pages — any content you create or send in the app is stored to provide the service and is visible to other users as intended by the feature.
Usage

Follows, ratings, reports, blocks — actions you take toward other users are stored to provide those features.
Notifications

Push notification token — collected when you grant notification permission, used only to deliver in-app notifications to your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide the core social features (Bubbles, Events, Pages, Chat)
  • To show your location to Bubble members when you opt in
  • To send you push notifications you have enabled
  • To enforce community safety (reports, blocks, moderation)
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and real-time messaging. Privacy Policy
  • Expo — app infrastructure and push notifications. Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign-In / Google OAuth — optional authentication methods governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap — used for address search when creating a Bubble. Only the address text you type is sent; no personal data is attached. Privacy Policy

4. Data Sharing

Your profile, content, and activity are visible to other users of the app as described by each feature. We do not share your personal data with any third party except as required to operate the services listed above or as required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account exists. Bubbles and their associated messages are deleted when the host ends the Hangout. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us (see Section 8).

6. Children's Privacy

The Hangout is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data via the Profile screen in the app
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time via your device settings
  • Disable location sharing at any time from within any Bubble
